# Clinical Operations KPI Definitions
Each KPI includes formula, interpretation, importance, and actions.

- **Enrollment Attainment %** = Randomized / Enrollment Target. Low values indicate recruitment risk; action: deploy support to underperforming sites.
- **Activation Rate** = Activated Sites / Planned Sites. Low rate indicates start-up bottlenecks; action: review contract/regulatory handoffs.
- **Query SLA Compliance %** = Queries resolved within SLA / total queries. Low compliance suggests data quality process issues; action: triage high-priority open queries.
- **Document Completion %** = Approved / Required docs. Low completion harms inspection readiness; action: focus on critical document remediation.
- **Avg Cycle Days** compares process duration vs target; high values indicate inefficiency; action: redesign delayed process steps.
- **Average Adoption Score** reflects digital behavior (usage + training + recency); low scores indicate platform adoption risk; action: targeted enablement.
