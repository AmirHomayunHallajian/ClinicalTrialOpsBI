# Business Questions by Stakeholder
## Executive Leadership
- Are studies meeting enrollment targets?
- Where are major operational bottlenecks?
## Clinical Operations Manager
- Which sites are delayed in activation?
- Which processes exceed target cycle time?
## Country Lead
- Which countries have highest missing docs and overdue queries?
## CRA / Site Monitor
- Which sites need immediate follow-up for recruitment/query backlog?
## Data Manager
- Are queries resolved within SLA and by priority?
## Document / TMF Specialist
- Which critical documents are missing or overdue by site/document type?
